QRevy v0.19.2

QRevy, formerly QR Repair, helps recover damaged QR codes offline on your Android device.

WHAT'S NEW
• See live candidate counts while recovery is running.
• Keep the complete cell selection after bulk edits, including unchanged cells, and restore the selection with Undo/Redo.
• Use provisional alignment guides before assigning Finder roles and the QR code's logical orientation.
• Correct bounded errors in single-segment terminator and padding bits while checking final candidates against the original observations and fixed-cell constraints.
• When an eligible failed search indicates that forcing a single segment may help, retry once without changing the saved preference. Saved history preserves that run's condition.
• Expanded the English and Japanese recovery-strategy documentation and updated dependencies and build tools.

SAVED WORK COMPATIBILITY
The Android app identity and saved-work location are unchanged.
This release writes QRR format version 27 and reads versions 23–27. Earlier files use defaults for settings they do not contain: a 12-bit partial-bit search limit for version 23, mirrored input off for versions 23–24, a list-decoding extension of +1 for versions 23–25, and one-time single-segment forcing off for versions 23–26. Other format versions cannot be opened.
QRevy v0.19.1 writes format version 26 and cannot open work saved or resaved in version 27.

REQUIREMENTS AND LIMITS
• Android 8.0 or later; QR Code Model 2, versions 1–40.
• Model 1, Micro QR, and rMQR are unsupported.
• Check the estimated QR outline, version, and Finder roles before repair. Difficult images may need manual adjustment.
• Importing an image or text matrix automatically saves work. At most 20 items are retained; exceeding the limit deletes the least recently saved work and its source image.

IMPORTANT
Recovery does not guarantee that the result matches the original content. Confidence scores are explanatory measures, not probabilities. Independently verify important information. Recovered web addresses (URLs) never open automatically.
