QR Repair v0.18.0

QR Repair helps recover damaged QR codes offline on your Android device.

WHAT'S NEW
• Added list decoding to search for additional candidates beyond the usual error-correction range. Candidates must still pass the selected constraints and QR validity checks before their content is reported as decoded.
• List decoding has a cumulative limit of 10 minutes per repair, excluding ordinary search time. Interrupting a repair or reaching a time or memory limit keeps candidates already found.
• Text matrix import assigns Finder roles and orientation automatically when the square finder patterns and surrounding structure identify a clear orientation. If roles remain unassigned, assign at least one before repair.
• Result diagnostics explain how candidates were recovered and whether the search finished, stopped early, or left calculations unresolved.

SAVED WORK COMPATIBILITY
Work is saved in QR Repair's QRR format, version 26. Versions 23–26 can be opened. Earlier files use defaults for settings they do not contain: a 12-bit partial-bit search limit for version 23, mirrored input off for versions 23–24, and a list-decoding extension of +1 for versions 23–25. Other format versions cannot be opened.

REQUIREMENTS AND LIMITS
• Android 8.0 or later; QR Code Model 2, versions 1–40.
• Model 1, Micro QR, and rMQR are unsupported.
• Check the estimated QR outline, version, and Finder roles before repair. Difficult images may need manual adjustment.
• Importing an image or text matrix automatically saves work. At most 20 items are retained; exceeding the limit deletes the least recently saved work and its source image.

IMPORTANT
Recovery does not guarantee that the result matches the original content. Confidence scores are explanatory measures, not probabilities. Independently verify important information. Recovered web addresses (URLs) never open automatically.
