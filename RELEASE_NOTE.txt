QR Repair v0.17.1

QR Repair helps recover damaged QR codes that ordinary readers cannot decode. Processing runs entirely offline on your Android device.

GETTING STARTED
1. Import an image, take a photo, or load a QR matrix text file.
2. Rotate, invert, adjust brightness or contrast, and crop as needed. Check the estimated corners, QR version, and black/unknown/white thresholds.
3. Review the positions and roles of the Finder patterns.
4. If needed, edit individual cells: 0 = white, 1 = black, ? = unknown. Use Tentative for uncertain edits and Fixed for values that recovery should preserve.
5. Tap Repair, then inspect the decoded content, alternative candidates, and explanations for repaired cells.

WHAT'S NEW
• Treat mirrored QR images as mirrored in Detailed settings. Finder roles and grid axes exchange while manual edits, fixed constraints, and Undo/Redo history follow the same image cells. This setting is saved per session and is OFF by default.
• Additional-segment diagnostics compare possible starting positions after a terminator, including byte alignment across known zero bits. Inspect segment and control-header traces, ECI assignments, declared lengths, parsing failures, raw tail bits, and HEX. Completed segments remain visible when later parsing fails. This diagnostic analysis does not make a rejected candidate valid.
• Unmask failed recovery attempts in the enlarged result view when an attempt provides a mask number. The mask source is shown; a failed attempt's mask is unverified.
• Improved initial grid estimation and thresholds for low-contrast QR regions and partially cropped outer edges. Transparent image imports are composited onto white before analysis.
• Corrected rectified previews and grid coordinates when changing QR versions or resizing images. After failed grid estimation or rectification, assign at least one Finder role before repair.
• Pending saves are preserved when switching work or starting another import. Repeated saves retain the latest requested history and selected checkpoint.
• Whitespace-only recovered text is preserved in result displays and comparisons, and single-segment trial diagnostics are clearer.
• Saved work now uses QRR schema 25. Schemas 23 and 24 can also be opened with mirrored input OFF; schema 23 uses the default 12-bit partial-bit search limit. Other schemas cannot be opened, and sessions in the old storage location are not migrated.
• The English and Japanese READMEs include an independently generated damaged-QR recovery example with reproducible inputs and results.

USEFUL FEATURES
• Undo/Redo, detailed cell diagnostics, and recovered-image export.
• Save work without running recovery, and resume it from Saved work (Recent work in Classic).
• English/Japanese interfaces and Classic/Modern layouts.

REQUIREMENTS AND LIMITS
• Android 8.0 or newer.
• Supports QR Code Model 2, versions 1–40.
• Model 1, Micro QR, and rMQR are unsupported.
• Strong perspective distortion is not supported.
• Imports automatically save work. At most 20 sessions are retained; exceeding the limit deletes the least recently saved work and its source image.

IMPORTANT
Recovery does not guarantee that the result matches the original content. Confidence scores are heuristics, not probabilities. Independently verify important information. Recovered URLs never open automatically.
