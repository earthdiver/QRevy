# Security

[日本語](SECURITY.ja.md)

A recovered candidate is not guaranteed to match the original content. Independently verify important information. Decoded content is displayed as text; embedded addresses are never opened automatically.

Workspace files are checked for valid format and integrity when imported. These checks do not establish who supplied a file or whether its contents are authentic. When attaching a source image to a QRR, verify that it is the corresponding image.

Serve the app over HTTPS and apply the supplied `dist/_headers` settings or their equivalent on your host. Keep your browser and operating system up to date.

Do not include private QR codes or photos in public bug reports. Provide a sample that reproduces the issue. See [Privacy](PRIVACY.md) for information about stored data.
