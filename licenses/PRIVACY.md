# Privacy

[日本語](PRIVACY.ja.md)

Photos and QR contents are processed on your device and are not sent to a server for recovery. The app does not collect usage analytics or track users. Initial loading and updates require a connection.

Workspaces and source images are stored separately for each browser and site address. Storage holds up to 20 workspaces with 40 history states each; older entries are removed when limits are exceeded. Browser data clearing or storage pressure can also remove them. See the [User Guide](docs/USER_GUIDE.en.md#7-save-resume-and-export) for saving and exporting work.

The app does not provide its own encryption or guarantee exclusion from device or browser backups. Exported files follow the destination's storage and sharing settings. Copied content is placed on the clipboard.

Image imports use the files you select. Photography uses the device and browser's camera facilities. Decoded addresses are never opened automatically.
